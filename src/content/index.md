# Kia ora,
I am a musician, music teacher and music administrator residing in Ōtepoti Dunedin. With more than five years teaching experience including cello, violin and theory lessons, I love helping people connect with music, finding out what it feels like to play and making lessons work for them.

I am a big believer in the joy of orchestral music and am grateful to support the Dunedin orchestral scene, as well as Dunedin music more widely. Music has shaped Dunedin as my home since moving here for study at the University of Otago.

Please click through to the about page to find out more!
